About Hope Life Foundation

Our background, core principles, and leadership team.

Our Story

Founded in July 2018 by social work graduates in New Delhi, Hope Life Foundation started with a simple weekend food drive. Over the past eight years, we have expanded to serve 38 communities through clean drinking water plants, primary school supplies, and medical screening camps.

Our Mission

To alleviate poverty by securing four foundational rights: clean water, quality schooling, regular meals, and basic healthcare.

Our Vision

A society where no family suffers from preventable waterborne illness, and where every child receives the tools required to complete education.

Core Values

Transparency

All donations and expenditures are audited publicly each year.

Community Partnership

Projects are planned and managed with local village councils.

Equality

Aid is delivered fairly to all people in need without discrimination.
